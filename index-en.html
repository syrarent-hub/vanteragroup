Monthly car rental in Athens

Monthly car rental is for anyone who wants a Volkswagen, BMW or Mercedes without tying up capital or signing a five-year leasing contract. You pay a fixed monthly amount, drive for as long as you need and stop whenever you want.

Why not leasing?

Leasing locks you in: three to five years, a down payment, a credit check and a penalty if you leave early. Vantera does the opposite:

  • No long-term commitment. Minimum term: one month.
  • No down payment. Only a refundable deposit.
  • No credit check. Just your licence and ID.
  • No exit penalty. Stop with simple notice.

Why not buy?

A new Mercedes GLC costs more than €55,000 and loses much of its value in the first three years. With Vantera, the same car costs €1,850 per month with everything included — and we keep the depreciation risk.

Who is it for?

  • Executives and entrepreneurs who want a premium car without tying up capital.
  • Companies that want an executive fleet without fleet management.
  • Visitors and expats staying in Athens for months who do not want to buy.
  • Anyone waiting for a new car delivery and needing a comparable car in the meantime.
  • Special occasions — weddings, events, filming and client hospitality.

Can’t find the model you want?

The fleet you see is a starting point, not a limit. We have access to the entire market — tell us the make, model and term and we will send you a quote.

Where we deliver

We cover all of Athens and Attica: the city centre, Kolonaki, the northern and southern suburbs, Piraeus and Athens International Airport. Delivery and collection take place at the address you provide, at no extra charge.
